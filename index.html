About

I am a Research Engineer at Energised Futures powered by Centrica, where I am taking my PhD research further and applying it to real-world energy systems. My work spans residential energy management, energy flexibility, electricity markets and optimisation — with a continued focus on making data-driven methods work outside the lab.

I completed my PhD at the AI4Energy research group (IDLab, UGent–imec), working at the intersection of reinforcement learning, explainable AI and forecasting for the energy sector. My dissertation, Deployable data-driven control algorithms for residential demand response, centred on one theme: practicality. I specialised in designing model architectures that stay robust under realistic constraints, such as limited data availability and the need for interpretability.

Research

My work spans model-free reinforcement learning, physics-informed neural networks and other specialised neural architectures for sample-efficient and interpretable controller design.
